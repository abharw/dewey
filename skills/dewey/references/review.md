# Review

Use this reference after consequential work, at a milestone, when progress stalls, when evidence conflicts with an earlier judgment, or when the learner's purpose or constraints change. Review at these meaningful points; ordinary turns need only the feedback that helps learning continue. [Teaching](teaching.md) owns the next instructional move; return to [Discovery](discovery.md) when the destination or starting assumptions need reconsideration.

## Establish what the evidence says

Compare the intended performance with what actually happened. Keep these separate:

- **Observation:** the learner's actual prediction, explanation, action, judgment, or revision, with its situation and source. Attribute self-reports as reports.
- **Assistance:** what a tutor, tool, AI, reference, or other person supplied, including cues that revealed the decisive step.
- **Interpretation:** what that contribution may support, why, and what remains uncertain or contradictory.

Include conditions that matter: task scope, available information, tools and references, guidance, and whether the learner selected or merely followed the approach. Permitted AI use can be part of the intended capability. A successful artifact establishes a result; inspect the learner's contribution before inferring capability from it.

Consider plausible alternatives when interpreting difficulty or success: unclear wording, interface friction, a slip, a leading hint, or repetition of a supplied explanation. Seek further evidence when it would materially change the next decision; otherwise retain the uncertainty and continue helping.

## Scope capability claims conservatively

[Vocabulary](vocabulary.md) owns the level definitions. Apply them through a warranted claim about particular behavior under particular conditions:

- **Familiar:** identify the learner's contribution that reveals a useful relationship or initial model. Hearing an explanation alone leaves understanding unobserved.
- **Capable:** identify how the learner accomplished the bounded purpose and judged the relevant result, including assistance. Keep the claim within that task's demands.
- **Fluent:** look for justified adaptation across changes that matter to the capability. Explain what changed and what the learner noticed or revised. Tutor-supplied adaptation supports an assisted result; broad flexibility remains unobserved.
- **Expert:** require evidence of sustained judgment across the domain's unfamiliar and poorly specified problems. A strong local performance warrants a local claim; it cannot establish this breadth by itself.

Choose evidence appropriate to the claim: later observations for retention, relevant different contexts for broader transfer, reduced cues when independent reasoning matters. Variation serves an inference, not a compulsory test. These labels have no validated universal threshold. Preserve unobserved dimensions as unknown; concrete progress can be recorded without changing a label.

## Revise the direction and account

Use the evidence to continue, advance, narrow, revisit, combine, or replace milestones. Explain changes that affect the learner's path. Targets reflect intended capability; current assessments reflect evidence. Change the target when purpose changes, rather than automatically matching it to current performance.

Update milestone status and progress using [Record formats](records.md#learning-path-and-milestones), linking the observations that justify the change. Read [Learning paths](learning-paths.md) for substantial changes to scope, dependencies, or sequence. Evaluate the goal's combined capability as well as its individual milestones.

Record what satisfied a completed goal's actual purpose and its source: observed work, a usable result, or the learner's stated satisfaction, as appropriate. An answered question may be complete without demonstrating retention. Goal completion, proficiency, and session ending are separate decisions.

Apply consequential updates through [Workspace and sessions](workspace.md) and [Record formats](records.md). Correct affected interpretations when source observations change. Keep proposed work distinct from attempted work, and preserve the unresolved question or next useful step honestly. A review can finish while learning continues.

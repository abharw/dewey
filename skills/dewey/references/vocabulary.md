# Working vocabulary

Use these distinctions to guide decisions. Learners can express their intentions in ordinary language; they do not need to select labels.

**Profile:** what we know about the person—their relevant context, interests, goals, preferences, and observations. Keep source and date; distinguish reports, evidence, and inference. Their profile is revisable and may initially be sparse.

**Pedagogy:** how the agent supports learning. Approaches can blend:

| Approach | Engagement |
|---|---|
| Explanatory | Explain, demonstrate, or walk through an example. |
| Socratic | Help the learner examine assumptions and reason through questions. |
| Experiential | Learn by doing, observing results, reflecting, and trying again. |

Methods such as analogies, examples, hints, comparison, and feedback can serve any approach.

**Evidence:** what the learner actually contributes. Explain, Predict, Apply, Adapt, and Judge are useful dimensions to notice, not a required checklist. Session records preserve the observations, situation, and assistance. Assessment interprets that evidence to decide what may be understood and what could help next; teaching and assessment have distinct purposes.

**Horizon:** the timeframe of a goal—Immediate (this interaction), Short-term (days or weeks), Long-term (months or longer). Preserve actual deadlines when known. Leave it unspecified when unknown. Timeframe does not determine proficiency, and an Immediate question can belong to a Long-term goal.

**Proficiency:** a claim about capability within a specific scope, supported by evidence. Goals hold target proficiency; topic proficiency records hold current scoped assessments. A topic can contain several assessments with different conditions and levels. An interest alone does not establish a target label.

| Level | Claim |
|---|---|
| Familiar | A useful initial mental model. |
| Capable | Can accomplish a bounded purpose and judge the relevant result. |
| Fluent | Can reason and act flexibly across meaningful variations. |
| Expert | Sustained judgment in a domain, including unfamiliar, poorly specified problems. |

AI assistance may be appropriate at every level; identify what the learner decided, checked, predicted, or revised. Scope a claim to the actual task and conditions. One polished explanation or successful generated artifact does not establish broad expertise. Unexplored means insufficient evidence, not inability. These are Dewey's working definitions, not a validated measurement scale.
